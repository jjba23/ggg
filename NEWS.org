* 1.2.0 (2026-11-12)

** Added

- Two new finishes: =crystal= and =crystal-mono=. A warm light is blurred
  behind a translucent plate, which is rounded into a capsule and rimmed with a
  bright hairline, so the sections of a badge glow through frosted glass instead
  of being painted over. The frost is faintest where the label sits, so a badge
  keeps the contrast its colors were chosen for, and the backlight only warms
  the corners.
- =crystal-mono= is the same glass with the labels set in a monospace font. A
  monospace advance is about a tenth wider than the sans widths a badge is
  measured with, so the style sets its type a tenth smaller: the label keeps its
  padding and the canvas stays the one every other style uses, so a row of the
  two variants still lines up.
- A style can now be a type variant: the skin contract grew =font-family=, which
  wins over the font a section asked for, and =font-scale=, which sets the
  labels smaller without touching the measured geometry.
- =glyphs/jjba23.scm= gains a =Crystal glass= group: eight of its badges again,
  four in =crystal= and four in =crystal-mono=, with accent sides drawn from
  Doom, Dracula, Dracula Alucard, Solarized, Modus Operandi, Modus Vivendi and
  the macchiato and frappe Catppuccin flavors.
- =glyphs/showcase.scm= shows both new finishes in its =Styles= group, and the
  gallery carries the new badges like any other.

** Documentation

- ~README.org~ documents =crystal= and =crystal-mono= in the style table, what
  the two variants are for, and the =Crystal glass= group of the project badges.
- ~resources/help.txt~ lists the two new finishes.

* 0.21.0 (2026-11-09)

** Added

  system preference, remembers the choice, and repaints the page in the

* 0.20.0 (2026-11-09)

** Added

- The badge builder has a light theme: the header toggles between the dark and
  a light Catppuccin Latte palette, starts from the system preference and
  remembers the choice.
- The gallery wears the project mark in its header and ends with the same
  footer as the builder: version, build revision, source, README, API docs, the
  badge builder, logo credits and the MIT License.

* 0.19.0 (2026-11-09)

** Added

- The badge builder autocompletes the logo library and every theme color, moves
  through the suggestions with the keyboard, shows one preview per finish,
  offers starting points (CI build, license, coverage, docs, Frutiger Aero,
  neon) and copies the URL, the Markdown or the HTML snippet.
- =GET /logo.svg?name=NAME= serves one logo from the library, so the builder can
  show a thumbnail beside each suggestion.
- =GET /logo.svg= serves the project's new mark, which the builder wears in its
  header and footer and links to the gallery from its header.
- The builder page ends with a footer naming the version, the build revision,
  the MIT License, the source code, the README, the API docs, the gallery and
  the logo credits.

** Changed

- The default corner radius grows from 3px to 3.5px (drawn 3.82px), so badges
  with the default corners sit slightly softer; ~square~ keeps its square
  corners and the finishes that round further keep their own radius.

** Documentation

- ~README.org~ and ~resources/help.txt~ document the logo routes, the builder's
  autocompletion, finish cards, starting points, gallery link and footer.

* 0.17.0 (2026-11-09)

** Added

- The badge server: =ggg --serve= answers =GET /badge.svg= with an SVG badge
  described by URL parameters (=text=, =label=, =logo=, =style=, =bg=, =fg= and
  their label counterparts). It binds to =127.0.0.1= by default, caches a
  successful badge for five minutes, and answers an invalid request with HTTP
  400 and every problem it found instead of a broken image.
- Badges with any number of sections from a URL: repeat =text=, and the Nth
  =bg= and =fg= pair with the Nth section. A URL describes up to 10 sections of
  100 characters each.
- A self-contained badge builder at the server's root path, with a live
  preview, add and remove section buttons and a copyable URL.

** Documentation

- ~README.org~ and ~resources/help.txt~ document the server, its parameters, the
  builder and the =--serve=, =--port= and =--host= flags.
